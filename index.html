04 — IDEAS IN PRACTICE

Projects & academic experience.

A collection of academic and collaborative projects focused on identifying real-world challenges, developing practical ideas and exploring innovative solutions. Across these projects, I have taken a leading role in ideation, research, planning and team collaboration.

01 / FINANCIAL LITERACY↗

MoneyMate

Learn Money Before You Earn It

A student-focused financial literacy platform concept for teenagers aged 15–18. It aims to make money management accessible through interactive lessons, real-life examples, budgeting tools and beginner-friendly investment simulations.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Led problem identification, target-audience research and concept development. Contributed to ideation, platform planning, business model development and overall project strategy.

02 / EDTECH · CAREER READINESS↗

North Star

From Potential to Proof

An experiential EdTech platform concept designed to bridge undergraduate education and professional expectations through four pillars: Learn, Experience, Connect and Launch. Developed as part of Boardroom Battle.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Led research, problem identification, ideation and strategic planning. Contributed to the platform concept, business model, project direction and team presentation.

03 / EVENT MANAGEMENT↗

Somaiya Unplugged

Pause. Connect. Recharge.

A campus-wide event concept centred on emotional awareness and student well-being, bringing together creative activities, games, music, nostalgia and reflection through themed interactive zones.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Initiated the event concept and helped shape its structure. Led ideation of interactive zones and activities, developing the event experience and central theme with the team.

04 / SUSTAINABILITY · PRODUCT INNOVATION↗

BrewTile

Coffee Waste. Sustainable Spaces.

A sustainable product concept exploring the reuse of discarded coffee grounds to create flooring tiles. The project progressed from concept development to a physical flooring-tile prototype.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Led concept development and coordinated the team's work towards the prototype. Contributed to ideation, planning, product development discussions and presentation.

05 / WASTE REPURPOSING↗

Reformed

From Waste to Purpose.

A sustainability-focused concept exploring the repurposing of discarded cigarette filters into acoustic wall panels. The concept resulted in a physical wall-panel prototype.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Contributed to the product concept and waste-repurposing approach, coordinating the team's work towards the prototype and leading planning, ideation and presentation.

06 / BUSINESS & ENVIRONMENT↗

SoilBoost

Reviving Soil Naturally.

A natural soil-nutrient blend concept responding to declining soil fertility and dependence on chemical fertilisers. The proposed blend combines plant ash, compost powder, neem leaf powder, eggshell powder and banana peel powder.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Contributed to developing and presenting the concept, including problem identification, product idea and proposed business and sustainability dimensions.
